What Our Servicing Includes

Filter Cleaning

Dirty filters restrict airflow and make your AC work harder. We clean and clear all filters for better performance and air quality.

Gas Check & Refill

Low refrigerant gas is a common cause of poor cooling. We check gas levels and refill where necessary.

Coil Cleaning

We clean both the evaporator and condenser coils to improve cooling efficiency and reduce energy consumption.

Drainage Check

Blocked drainage pipes cause water leaks. We inspect and clear drainage to prevent damage to your unit and property.

Electrical Inspection

We check all electrical connections, capacitors and components to ensure your AC is running safely.

Full System Test

After servicing we run a complete test to confirm your AC is cooling properly before we leave.
