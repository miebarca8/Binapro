Our Uninstallation Process

Safe Gas Recovery

Before removal we safely recover any remaining refrigerant gas from the unit to prevent leaks and environmental damage.

Electrical Disconnection

We properly disconnect all electrical connections and isolate the power supply before touching any components.

Indoor Unit Removal

We carefully remove the indoor unit from the wall bracket without damaging your wall surface or paintwork.

Outdoor Unit Removal

The outdoor compressor unit is safely detached and removed from its mounting position — whether wall, floor or rooftop mounted.

Pipe & Cable Removal

We remove all copper pipes, drainage pipes and electrical cables cleanly and neatly to leave your space tidy.

Site Cleanup

After removal we clean up the work area and seal any wall openings left behind by the installation pipes.
