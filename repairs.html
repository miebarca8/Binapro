Common AC Problems We Fix

AC Not Cooling

If your AC is running but not producing cold air, it could be low gas, a dirty filter or a faulty compressor. We diagnose and fix it fast.

Water Leaking

Water dripping from your AC is usually caused by a blocked drain pipe or dirty evaporator coil. We clear blockages and stop the leak.

Strange Noises

Grinding, rattling or clicking sounds from your AC indicate loose parts or a failing motor. We identify the source and fix it properly.

AC Not Turning On

Electrical faults, blown fuses or faulty capacitors can stop your AC from powering on. We check all electrical components and restore power.

Compressor Issues

The compressor is the heart of your AC. We repair or replace faulty compressors to restore full cooling performance.

Remote & Control Issues

If your AC is not responding to the remote or controls, we check the receiver board and fix communication issues quickly.
