Our Gas Recharge Process

System Diagnosis

We first check your AC system pressure and cooling performance to confirm that low gas is actually the problem before proceeding.

Leak Detection

Before adding gas we check for leaks in the pipes and connections. Filling gas into a leaking system is a waste of money.

Leak Repair

If we find a leak we fix it first before recharging ensuring the gas stays in the system and your AC keeps cooling long term.

Gas Recharge

We refill your AC with the correct refrigerant gas R22, R410A or R32 depending on your AC model and manufacturer specifications.

Pressure Check

After recharging we verify the pressure is at the correct level for your specific AC unit to ensure maximum cooling efficiency.

Performance Test

We run a full cooling test after recharging to confirm your AC is back to producing cold air at the right temperature.

Signs Your AC Needs Gas Recharge

✔ AC running but not cooling
✔ Takes too long to cool the room
✔ Ice forming on the pipes
✔ Hissing sound from the unit
✔ Higher electricity bills than usual
✔ AC blowing warm or room temperature air
