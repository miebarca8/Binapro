Our Story

My journey into the HVAC industry started with my father, who spent years working as an air-conditioning technician. Growing up, he often encouraged me to join him on jobs, but at the time I had other interests and did not see it as something I would pursue long-term.

As the years went by, I gained hands-on experience by assisting him with installations, servicing, maintenance, and repairs. These experiences gave me practical knowledge of the trade and a strong understanding of how cooling systems work.

When I gained admission into the university, I explored different skills, including graphic design and video editing, hoping to create opportunities for myself. While those skills taught me valuable lessons, I eventually realized that one of the most useful skills I had learned was already with me.

I began taking on small HVAC jobs, starting with basic servicing and maintenance work. With guidance from my father, I gradually built my confidence, expanded my knowledge, and invested in my own tools. What started as a skill soon became a reliable source of income and a profession I genuinely enjoyed.

Over the years, I have worked on a variety of residential and commercial projects, gaining practical field experience and developing a reputation for reliability and quality workmanship. I have also worked as a freelance technician with HYLY, contributing my skills and experience to HVAC-related projects while continuing to serve clients independently.

Today, Binapro-HVAC Hub is built on professionalism, integrity, and a commitment to customer satisfaction. Every project is approached with attention to detail, whether it involves installation, servicing, repairs, or routine maintenance.

My goal is simple: to provide reliable cooling solutions that keep homes, offices, and businesses comfortable. I believe every customer deserves quality service, honest communication, and a technician who is committed to getting the job done right.

— Founder, Binapro HVAC Hub
